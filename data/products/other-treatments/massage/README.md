# massage

Empty, and deliberately so.

Eminence has no massage product line. The nearest thing is their body oils,
and those are already collected one folder across in `../body/`
(`body-lotions-oils.json`, 11 products).

Copying them here would give two files that drift apart the next time either
is rescraped. If massage ever needs its own shelf, pick the oils out of body
by SKU rather than duplicating the file.
