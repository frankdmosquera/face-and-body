The whole deal

face-and-body

The first website for Face and Body Wellness Centre, a medical aesthetics clinic in Midnapore, Calgary SE. Live at faceandbodywellnesscentre.com. This page is the project's status board: where things stand, in a few seconds, without reading a transcript.

From the project plan

1 · Problem
She had no website. Her whole web presence was a bare Square booking menu of 45 services with no photographs and no reason to choose her. High-intent searches like "microneedling Calgary" found nothing, her Google hours were wrong on six days of seven, and her Eminence stockist status and 5.0 reviews were visible nowhere. A first website, not a redesign.
2 · Users
A woman in southeast Calgary, roughly 30 to 55, with a specific skin concern, comparing two or three local clinics on her phone at night. Then the local regular who needs the fastest path to booked, and the gift buyer who does not know what any treatment is. Assumed, not yet confirmed with the clinic.
3 · Features
Home page, treatments, correct seven-day hours with an open-now state, text-first contact, booking on our own pages, local SEO. Since Sep 20 facials rank from the home page and the rest sit on /other-treatments; /products was added Sep 21. Out of scope: online sales, client accounts, consent forms, a blog.
4 · Data
No database. Typed data in the repo: 40 treatments in servicesData.ts, one siteConfig for address, phone and hours, 62 hand-carried reviews, and the Eminence catalogue (194 records for 152 products, images gitignored). Bookings live in Cal.com; nothing about a visitor is stored.
5 · Tech
Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, shadcn. ImageKit for every photograph, Resend for the contact form, Cal.com behind one booking component. The unused auth and database packages were removed Sep 20.
6 · Monetize
The site makes no money directly; success is booked appointments. The commercial arrangement for the build and its upkeep is still unverified.
7 · UI/UX
Warm editorial with clinic credibility. Copper #c0704f from her logo on a settled cream, Cormorant Garamond with Inter, the orchid from her logo as watermark and divider, motion in CSS only. Mobile first: the main visitor is on a phone at night.
8 · Deployment
Vercel, deployed on every push to main, on her own domain since Sep 21. Four environment variables, listed under Architecture. The old face-and-body.vercel.app address answers 404.

Locked decisions

  • A marketing site, nothing more. No database, no auth, no backend. Her booking, store and dashboards will be built as scheduleads modules, never here.
  • Booking sits behind one component. No Cal.com call or id appears outside components/booking/, so replacing Cal.com with scheduleads later changes one place.
  • Facials rank from the home page. Decided Sep 20, reversing Sep 18: on a new domain only the home page has authority, and two similar pages make Google pick neither. Five pages total; branch out only once the home page ranks.
  • One source for her facts. Address, phone and hours live in siteConfig only, and hours always render as seven rows, never collapsed into ranges.
  • Products in the site's own words. Seven headings matching the facial tabs, Eminence's Canadian list prices, spa-only products kept off the page, no per-product pages yet.
  • Reviews are hand-carried. The live Google fetch is built and deliberately unused; Google caps it at five and the curated set reads better.
  • Every photograph on ImageKit, with only the two logos kept in public/.
  • The business speaks as "we". No apologising, no explaining the obvious.
  • No .env.example, ever. A file that pairs a key name with a blank invites a secret into git.

Waiting on the clinic

Not code. Each of these holds back quality or a feature, and each is a question for her rather than work for us. Hours and the postal code, once on this list, are settled.

Photography
The biggest one. No wide shot of the room, no portrait of her, no product shelf, one usable before and after. Stock photographs stand in until real ones arrive. Feature 12 is blocked on it entirely.
The logo file
All we have is her 150px Instagram avatar. The favicon and the header both came from it; the original vector or a large PNG would sharpen both.
Missing prices
Laser, IPL and fractional microneedling still have no price anywhere.
Copy sign-off
Treatment descriptions were rewritten from supplier marketing and need her approval, especially anything medical. No treatment has a story of its own yet, which is what feature 5 is really waiting on.
Advertising claims
Detox, fat-reduction and cellulite language from her Square copy, not yet checked against Alberta's rules.
About page facts
Her name as she wants it published, her qualification, when she started, a photograph. Feature 7 needs all four.

How it is built

Architecture

What the site talks to, every page it serves, where each part of the code lives, and what it reads from the environment. A change that adds a page, a folder or a service updates this tab when it is published.

What it talks to

The pages

/
Home, and the page that ranks for facials: hero, trust strip, the 22 facials in four tabbed groups, Eminence, reviews, FAQ, consultation. app/page.tsx
/other-treatments
The other 18: skin, massage, body, laser. app/other-treatments/page.tsx
/products
The Eminence catalogue in seven groups, with search, sorting and sticky group chips. app/products/page.tsx
/contact
Text first, then call, then the form through Resend. Hours and location live here too, since Sep 19. app/contact/page.tsx
/book
One booking page for every treatment, reading ?treatment=. Kept out of search on purpose. Most Book buttons open the overlay instead. app/book/page.tsx

Where things live

data/
Everything the pages say: servicesData, siteConfig, categoriesData, reviewsData, faqData, imagesData, productsData, and the parked concernsData.
components/
By area: home, treatments, products, contact, business (hours, open now, directions), booking (the one seam), layout, media, brand, theme, and shadcn's ui.
lib/
Small logic the pages share: hours and open-now, service and product queries, contact validation and prefills, booking config, the parked Google reviews fetch.
actions/
contactAction.ts, the only server code: validates the form and sends it through Resend.
blueprint/
The plans, the coding standards, this page's source (context/project-log.html), and every finished feature and fix under history/.

What it reads from the environment

Secrets live in .env.local and in Vercel's settings, nowhere else. grep -rn "process.env" app components lib data actions gives the four below, plus Vercel's own VERCEL flag.

NEXT_PUBLIC_IMAGEKIT_URL_ENDPOINT
Public by design, it shows in every image URL. Missing on Vercel, the build fails rather than serve broken images.
RESEND_API_KEY
Missing, every enquiry fails with the same generic error and nothing explains why.
RESEND_FROM
hello@faceandbodywellnesscentre.com, verified in Resend Sep 21.
GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY
Optional. Without it the reviews fall back to the hand-carried set, which is the intended state.
Not read
IMAGE_KIT_PRIVATE_KEY sits in .env.local with no code reading it, kept by your decision on Sep 20. CONTACT_TO was deleted Sep 21 when enquiries moved to the clinic address.

Roadmap

Eighteen planned features and four pieces of work that were never in the plan. The site is live and books appointments. Nothing is being built right now. States here follow what is actually on the site, which is ahead of the ticks in build-plan.md.

Open for you 4 open

None of these stops the site. Each is a decision or a loose end that only you can close.

  • Cal.com and Square can sell the same hour. Neither sees the other's bookings. Three ways out: move everything off Square on a date, connect both to one Google Calendar if Square writes to it, or narrow Cal.com's hours to ones Square is not selling.
  • The build plan is behind the site. Booking (11) went live Sep 21 but the file still says "not now", and 13 and 14 are mostly there. Its ticks are yours to update.
  • face-and-body.vercel.app answers 404. The real domain works. Likely harmless, not yet explained.
  • Resend receiving is half set up. Switched on without its MX records, so the domain reads partly verified. Sending is unaffected. Finish it or switch it off.
