This page is the project's status board. It answers where things stand in a few seconds, without reading a transcript. /overview writes this view and the roadmap from the two plan docs; every feature adds its own group as it starts.

From the project plan

1 · Problem
A licensed medical aesthetics clinic running since 2023 with no website. Its whole web presence is a bare Square menu: 45 services in one flat list, no photographs, no reason to choose her. Her $290 and $350 treatments are line items in a dropdown, and her Google profile publishes wrong hours on six of seven days.
2 · Users
Primary: a woman in SE Calgary, roughly 30–55, with a specific skin concern, comparison-shopping clinics on her phone at night. Secondary: the local regular rebooking every four to six weeks. Tertiary: the gift buyer. No accounts, every visitor anonymous.
3 · Features
Eleven Phase 1 features: design foundation, services data, home, category pages, treatment detail, browse by concern, about, hours and location, contact, SEO, and booking. Out of scope: product sales, accounts, consent forms, records, blog.
4 · Data
No database. Typed constants in the repo: Service, Concern, BusinessFacts, Review. Bookings live in Cal.com, deposits in Stripe. Nothing about a visitor is stored.
5 · Tech
Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, shadcn. ImageKit for images, Resend for the contact fallback, Cal.com for booking behind a swappable component. No database, no auth, no backend.
6 · Monetize
Not in v1. The site earns nothing directly; success is booked appointments. Commercially an agency client project, with the arrangement still unverified.
7 · UI/UX
Warm editorial with clinic credibility. Palette sampled from her real logo: copper #c0704f, cream #f7f2ea, near-black ink. Cormorant Garamond over Inter, the orchid as watermark, motion in CSS only. Mobile matters more than desktop.
8 · Deployment
Vercel, npm run build, single deploy unit. One env var, RESEND_API_KEY, for feature 9 only. No database, storage, workers or cron. No domain owned yet.

Locked decisions

Service shape
Features 3–11 and all of Phase 2 read it. durationMin blocks her calendar and price is legitimately null on three services.
Seven-day hours
Her hours differ every day and total about 24 hours a week. They render as seven explicit rows, never collapsed into ranges.
Booking is swappable
Cal.com is the first provider, not a commitment. No Cal.com import, call or id appears outside the booking component, so Frank’s own booker can replace it in one file.
Positioning
Medical aesthetics clinic, not wellness sanctuary. Results and expertise lead.
No database
Marketing site. Adding one is a project-plan change, not a feature decision.

Design foundation
Port prototypes/theme.css into globals.css @theme, fonts, orchid motif, nav and footer shell.
02
Business facts and services data
One typed module for hours, address and the 45 services. Everything downstream reads it.
03
Home page
Hero, trust strip, browse by concern, categories, signature treatment, results, Eminence, reviews, hours, location.
04
Treatment category pages
Five categories, grouped within each page, with concern filtering.
05
Treatment detail pages
One template for every service, deep for the high-value ones.
06
Browse by concern
Landing pages per concern, for visitors who know the problem and not the treatment name.
07
About page
Her story, credentials, the room, Eminence partnership.
08
Hours and location
Seven-day hours with an open-now state, map, directions, parking, click-to-call and text.
09
Contact and enquiry
Text-first, Resend form as fallback, and the ask-about-pricing path for treatments with no published price.
10
Local SEO foundations
LocalBusiness and Service schema, metadata, Open Graph, sitemap, robots.
11
Booking integration
One booking component with a service-shaped interface, Cal.com behind it, plus the event-type generator. The headline feature.

After-hours enquiry assistant
