What it is

Controlled injury, real repair

Microneedling uses a pen of fine, cone-shaped needles to make thousands of microscopic channels in the skin. Each one seals within minutes. The skin reads them as damage and responds the only way it knows how: by building new collagen and elastin.

Over the six to eight weeks that follow, that new tissue thickens the dermis, softens scars from underneath and fills fine lines. It's the same mechanism behind far more expensive laser resurfacing, without the heat and with a fraction of the downtime.

"Your skin does the work. We just give it the reason to."

Because the needles displace rather than cut, bruising and swelling are minimal. Most clients are pink for a day and back to normal by the second.

Suitable for

Who this is for

Acne scarring

Rolling and boxcar scars respond best. Ice-pick scars improve but need more sessions.

Fine lines and crepey skin

Around the eyes, mouth and neck, where the skin has thinned with age.

Stretch marks

On the abdomen especially. See the abdomen treatment below.

Pigmentation

Post-inflammatory marks and uneven tone, often paired with a peel.

Enlarged pores

Tightens the collagen around each pore so it reads smaller.

Overall texture

Skin that looks tired and rough under makeup, with no single cause.

Your visit

What happens in 90 minutes

Consult and cleanse

We look at your skin, agree the depth and the areas, and cleanse thoroughly.

Numbing

Topical anaesthetic for thirty minutes. This is most of the appointment time, and it's why the treatment itself is comfortable.

Treatment

Around twenty minutes of needling, with a hyaluronic serum gliding under the pen. Feels like light sandpaper.

Calm and protect

Soothing mask, then SPF. You leave pink, like mild sunburn. No makeup for 24 hours.

Questions

Before you book

Does it hurt?

With numbing, most people describe it as a light scratching. The cheeks and forehead are easy; the upper lip and around the nose are the spots people notice. Nobody has asked us to stop.

How many sessions do I need?

One session gives a visible glow and some tightening. Real change to scarring or lines takes three to six sessions, spaced four to six weeks apart, because collagen builds slowly. We'll be honest about which you need at your consultation.

What can't I do afterwards?

No makeup, sweating, sun, or active skincare for 24 hours. No retinol or acids for a week. Sunscreen every day for a month. It's simple, and skipping it is the main reason results disappoint.

Who should not have microneedling?

Active acne, open wounds, eczema flares, keloid scarring history, pregnancy, and anyone on isotretinoin in the last six months. We check all of this before we start.
