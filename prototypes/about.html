About the clinic

A clinic that looks at your skin before it looks at the menu

Face and Body Wellness Centre is an independent medical aesthetics clinic in Midnapore, Calgary SE. One owner, one room, forty-five treatments, and no upsell.

The owner

I opened this clinic because I was tired of watching people get sold a package before anyone had looked at their face.

Every appointment here starts the same way: I look at your skin under proper light, ask what's bothering you, and tell you what I think will work. Sometimes that's the treatment you came in for. Sometimes it's something cheaper. Occasionally it's nothing, and I'll say so.

The treatments are the ones I'd want done on my own skin. Microneedling because it's the most clinically supported thing on the list. Eminence Organics because I've used it for years and it doesn't wreck your barrier. Massage because your face doesn't sit on top of a body that's ignored.

Midnapore, not downtown, because this is the neighbourhood I live in and parking shouldn't be part of the stress.
