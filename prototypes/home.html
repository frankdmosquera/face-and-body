Questions

Before your first visit

The things people text us to ask. If yours isn't here, text it.

Do I need a consultation before booking a treatment?

No. If you know what you want, book it. The consultation is for people who don't, or who want a second opinion on a plan from somewhere else. It's free either way.

How do I book?

Everything runs through Square. Pick the treatment, pick the time, pay a deposit or pay in full, done. You'll get a text reminder the day before. Cancel free up to 24 hours ahead.

Where do I park?

Right outside. Unit 330 is in the plaza at 290 Midpark Way SE, and the lot is free. If you're coming from Macleod Trail, turn at Midlake Boulevard.

What should I do before a facial?

Arrive with a clean face if you can, no retinol the night before, and skip the gym right after. That's it. We'll cover anything treatment-specific at the time.
